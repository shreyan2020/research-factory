These were retrospective, assistant-reviewed checks on exposed material. They were not independent human validation, fresh held-out benchmarks, new clustering runs, or a live classroom test. The manuscript exercise did not establish full-paper compilation or scientific correctness. The original research files were preserved.

## Public-package checks

The five skill entry points, UI metadata, relative reference links, and structured templates were checked. Public lesson references summarize transferable workflow lessons without private source excerpts, local paths, or chat identifiers. Packaging checks establish navigability and structure, not empirical effectiveness.

## Repeating the useful checks

1. Freeze a task, source packet, output contract, and rubric before comparing variants.
2. Keep the original skill snapshot and baseline output. Use comparable inputs and budgets.
3. Inspect semantics against sources as well as structural validity. Record evaluator identity and exposure.
4. Change the guidance responsible for a concrete failure and replay the task. Report regressions and ties.
5. For a general improvement claim, add fresh cases and independent judgments; do not relabel development examples as held-out evidence.
