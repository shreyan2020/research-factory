# Methods borrowed from other domains

Research notes checked 14 November 2026. This is an original workflow adaptation, not a validated research-narrative method or a claim that these sources endorse it. The sources below motivate individual choices; the combination and boundaries are design judgments.

| Domain and source | Useful practice | Research adaptation | Where the analogy stops |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fiction outlining: Randy Ingermanson's [Snowflake Method](https://www.advancedfictionwriting.com/articles/snowflake-method/) | Expand a compact premise into progressively more detailed outlines | Expand a provisional takeaway into an argument paragraph, sections, and figure purposes | Do not preselect a successful ending, invent crises, or cast a baseline as a villain. Before results, outline conditional outcomes |
| Argumentation: [Purdue OWL's account of Toulmin](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/academic_writing/historical_perspectives_on_argumentation/toulmin_argument.html) | Distinguish a claim, evidence, the warrant connecting them, and qualifications or rebuttals | Put the inferential step and rival explanation beside each consequential claim | A convincing argument structure cannot turn weak evidence into strong evidence or establish causal identification |
| Program evaluation: Innovations for Poverty Action's [Theory of Change guide](https://data.poverty-action.org/monitoring-evaluation-learning/theory-of-change.html) | Make the assumptions behind proposed pathways explicit | Annotate mechanism links with conditions and the observations needed to assess them | A theory-of-change pathway is a proposed causal account. A research argument's support edge is not automatically a causal arrow |
| Scientific writing: Mensh and Kording, [Ten simple rules for structuring papers](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1005619), 2017 | Organize around a central contribution and use context, content, and conclusion at multiple scales | Build a reader sequence that establishes the question, supplies evidence, and explains its consequence | A central contribution may be multifaceted. Narrative focus does not license selective reporting or eliminate a material unresolved result |
| Open science: Center for Open Science, [Preregistration](https://www.cos.io/initiatives/prereg) | Preserve the distinction between planned and unplanned analyses and report departures | Keep a commitments register linked to the changing paper argument | A local protocol alone is not proof of registry submission; a retrospective narrative cannot confer confirmatory status |

## Why use three views?

A single diagram often conflates three different things: the decisions made during a study, the logical support for a conclusion, and the order of a paper. Keeping their relationships explicit lets the author improve exposition without fabricating a clean history.

The commitment view points to the existing protocol and amendments. The argument view answers “why should we believe this?” The reader sequence answers “what does the reader need to understand next?” Their IDs are shared, but their orders and edge meanings differ.

## Practices deliberately excluded

- Fixed act counts, hero journeys, and obligatory dramatic reversals. A mixed or inconclusive result may be the scientifically meaningful endpoint.
- Choosing the most surprising significant result as the root and treating the rest as expendable.
- Treating a neat diagram, agent consensus, or narrative score as validation of novelty or causal truth.
- Replacing a domain's evidence standards with generic storytelling rules.

## Lessons adapted from actual factory work

The following are generalized observations from prior work, not conclusions established by the external sources above:

- Rechecking close literature can weaken an initially attractive contribution. Compare rival framings and near precedents before writing a strong novelty claim.
- Agreement or disagreement can come from different evidence windows, selection rules, or measurement definitions. Inspect those before giving it a substantive interpretation.
- A sophisticated mechanism can tie a simpler same-information control. A defensible story must retain that comparison and may become a boundary-condition argument.
- The latest manuscript and explicit user decisions govern edits. Developing a framing does not itself authorize rewriting a coauthor's paper.
