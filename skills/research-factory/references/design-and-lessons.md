# Design and lessons

The coordinating structure was inspired by [Poteto mode](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/main/pstack/skills/poteto-mode/SKILL.md). This is an original research adaptation: a small coordinator, independently usable specialists, observable completion, and lessons from failures. It does not inherit another plugin's model defaults, agent types, permissions, or product-specific rituals.

## Why this structure

1. One coordinator and four specialists match recurring research tasks without requiring a four-stage pipeline.
2. Source identities, qualifiers, status, and denominators survive handoffs so writing does not promote preliminary work into findings.
3. Checks target the requested result: semantic support, method usefulness, manuscript meaning, or saved presentation usability.
4. Failed runs and direct baselines remain visible. Complexity must justify its cost.
5. Details are loaded only when relevant, keeping small tasks small.

The public edition retains generalized lessons; private artifacts and machine-specific provenance remain with their owner. These observations do not establish general model-performance improvements or current facts about other projects.

When adapting the factory, inspect the current owning evidence, retain a concrete failure case, change the responsible specialist, and repeat an appropriate task. Keep observed behavior, proposed process changes, and demonstrated improvement distinct.
